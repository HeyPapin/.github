# HeyPapin

**An AI tutor that explains out loud while drawing on a shared whiteboard.**

Most AI tutors answer in a wall of text. HeyPapin teaches the way a good teacher does at a board: it talks, draws boxes, arrows, equations and graphs as it goes, and lets you step in whenever you want.

## What you can do

- **Interrupt at any moment.** Ask a question mid-sentence and the tutor stops and answers.
- **Draw back.** Sketch, write a step, circle something or cross it out. HeyPapin notices and continues from your work.
- **Fix one step, not the whole board.** Edit a single equation or label where it stands.
- **Learn from your own material.** Upload a PDF, paper or lecture slides and turn it into a step-by-step visual lesson.
- **Study in your language.** English, Bahasa Indonesia, Bahasa Melayu, Hindi, Japanese and Chinese.
